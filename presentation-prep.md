Use this page to rehearse for the oral presentation section of the rubric.

## Presentation Structure (5-7 Minutes)

### 1) Opening (30-45 seconds)

- Introduce yourself and your focus area
- State one sentence on what this portfolio proves about your growth

Template:

"Hi, I am [name], and my portfolio highlights growth in [focus area]. Across these artifacts, I demonstrate development in [skill 1], [skill 2], and [skill 3]."

### 2) Portfolio Walkthrough (3-4 minutes)

Choose 3 to 4 strongest artifacts and explain each briefly:

- Context: what the artifact is
- Your role: what you specifically did
- Impact: result, learning, or outcome
- Reflection: why this matters for your future

### 3) Reflection Synthesis (1 minute)

Connect artifacts to a personal growth narrative:

- What changed in your thinking
- What changed in your process
- What changed in your confidence and communication

### 4) Closing (20-30 seconds)

End with a forward-looking statement:

"This portfolio captures where I started, how I grew, and the direction I am taking next in [field/goal]."

## Delivery Rubric Practice Checklist

### Audience Connection

- Keep your body orientation toward audience and reviewer
- Use natural pauses instead of reading directly from screen
- Show ownership: explain decisions in first person and with confidence

### Volume and Projection

- Rehearse once at conversation volume
- Rehearse once at presentation volume for the room
- Avoid speaking too fast; emphasize key phrases

### Responsiveness to Questions

Use this 3-step response pattern:

1. Clarify: "Great question. You're asking about [topic]."
2. Answer: give one concise direct response
3. Extend: give a specific example from an artifact

## High-Value Practice Questions

- Which artifact best represents your academic growth and why?
- What challenge taught you the most, and what changed after that?
- How do your artifacts show both technical and interpersonal development?
- If you had one more semester, what would you improve in this portfolio?

## Day-Of Readiness Check

- Open all artifact links before your presentation starts
- Keep one backup tab open for your reflection page
- Prepare 2-3 short stories with concrete outcomes
- Practice a strong first sentence and final sentence
